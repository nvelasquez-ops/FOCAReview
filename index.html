Game Rules

  • Every player starts with 12 flags: 3 blue, 3 magenta, 3 orange, 3 yellow.
  • Flags are worth an unknown number of points (revealed only at the end). Values range from −2 to +5 (no zeros).
  • Players choose a math category and solve a problem. Correct solvers (in random order) may steal one flag of any color from any other player.
  • If nobody is correct, each player loses one random flag they still own.
  • After every two regular problems, a random player is asked whether the group wants to play a Trivia round for a power-up. If they decline, the group must complete another math problem before a different player is asked.
  • Power-ups (only one player can win a trivia round):
    • 🔄 Redistribute a Color – All players lose every flag of a chosen color; the flags are redistributed as evenly as possible (remainder discarded).
    • 💥 Force 2 Flags – Choose one opponent and take any two of their flags (they become yours).
    • 🔮 Value Reveal – Privately see the point value of one color of your choice. Only you learn it.
  • You can end the game at any time with the red END GAME button at the bottom of the page.

Trivia Round – Rules

Honor system lock-in

1. Write your answer on a whiteboard or paper.
2. When ready, flip the board face-down and call out “First!”, “Second!”, “Third!”, etc.
3. Do not flip your board back up or you lose your spot.
4. After the answer is revealed, the group decides together (honor system) who locked in the correct answer first.
5. Only that player receives the power-up.
